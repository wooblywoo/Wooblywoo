Made for teams that share what they can't expose

Insurance, finance and consulting run on sensitive data moving between principals, counterparties and departments. Wooblywoo lets it move — on-demand, need-to-know, and provably.

🏥 Insurance

Claims, underwriting files and member data are pure PHI/PII — yet they constantly move to reinsurers, adjusters, TPAs and brokers. Wooblywoo shares them without copying regulated data into anyone else's systems.

  • Share claims files & medical records with reinsurers and adjusters — HIPAA-aligned, view-only, time-boxed
  • Send underwriting data to carriers/MGAs while masking fields outside their need-to-know
  • Revoke a TPA or broker's access the moment an engagement ends — including anything downloaded
  • Hand auditors a complete record of who viewed which member's data, when

🏦 Finance & banking

Deals, counterparties and internal walls all demand controlled disclosure. Wooblywoo enforces it cryptographically — GLBA, SOX and MNPI need-to-know without slowing the deal.

  • Run M&A / financing data rooms where the host never holds readable documents
  • Exchange KYC/AML and beneficial-ownership data with counterparties without bulk data transfer
  • Enforce information-barrier / MNPI need-to-know across desks and deal teams at the field level
  • Stream every decryption into your SIEM for GLBA and SOX evidence

📊 Consulting & advisory

Firms hold their clients' most sensitive material and must keep every engagement walled off — from each other and from unauthorized internal eyes. Wooblywoo makes confidentiality the default.

  • Exchange documents and models with principals and client stakeholders securely, no new portal for them
  • Keep each client engagement cryptographically isolated — no accidental cross-client exposure
  • Grant associates access scoped to a single workstream; revoke instantly at roll-off
  • Demonstrate airtight data handling in RFPs and client security reviews

Principals & cross-departmental orgs

Some of the hardest boundaries are internal. Wooblywoo enforces need-to-know across departments and up to principals — on the same document, without duplicate copies.

Principal ↔ team

Principals share sensitive files down to specific team members and pull access back the instant priorities change.

Cross-department

Legal, finance, HR and compliance exchange records where each side sees only the fields their role allows.

One source of truth

No emailed copies proliferating across inboxes — one encrypted asset, many scoped, revocable views.
